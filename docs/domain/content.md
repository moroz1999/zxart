## Static content pages

The About section carries editorial pages the site ships with rather than pages
anyone edits on the site: an introduction, a FAQ, a support page, and the
documentation of the public export API.

They are written per language. A page that has not been translated yet is shown
in English, so the section is never incomplete.

The FAQ opens with a table of contents linking to its own sections.
