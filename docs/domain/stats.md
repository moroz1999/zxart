## Statistics

The statistics section describes the archive as a whole and the people who build
it.

It opens with the totals of the collection, and then splits into software, music,
graphics and members.

### Per section
Each of the three collections is described the same way:

- how much of it there is, and how much of it is rated above the site's average
- how it grew, year by year, counted by the year the work was made
- how it divides up: software by top-level category, by the computer it needs and
  by country; music by format and country; graphics by format and country
- what happened over the last thirty days — programs uploaded, tunes played,
  pictures viewed
- who contributed the most of it

A work whose year of creation is unknown still counts towards the totals; it
simply cannot appear in a year series.

### Members
The most active voters, commenters and taggers, each with their name and a link
to their page.
